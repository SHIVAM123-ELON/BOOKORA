# BOOKORA — Google Play Store Release Preparation Guide

## 1. App Listing Information

- **App Name**: `Bookora: E-Book Reader & Store` (Limit: 30 chars)
- **Short Description**: `Discover, buy, and read best-selling e-books with AI reading assistance.` (Limit: 80 chars)
- **Full Description**:
```
Welcome to Bookora — the next-generation digital reading and publishing ecosystem designed for readers, students, and independent authors.

Key Features:
• Rich Digital Catalog: Explore thousands of e-books across Technology, Business, Education, Fiction, Competitive Exams, and Self-Help.
• Intelligent AI Reading Assistant: Powered by Gemini to provide chapter summaries, concept explanations, and study guides in real time.
• Offline Reading Mode: Download purchased books securely to read anytime, anywhere without an internet connection.
• Author Studio: Empowering independent writers with transparent royalty tracking, audience insights, and direct publishing tools.
• Safe & Secure Checkout: Supports UPI, Net Banking, Cards, and Bookora Wallet credits.
• Community & Growth: Share your favorite quotes, write reviews, and earn reading credits by inviting friends.

Discover your next favorite book today on Bookora!
```
